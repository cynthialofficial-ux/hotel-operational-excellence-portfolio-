Recommended decision

Run a controlled Moderate pilot, not a full-scale rollout.

Approve a staged pilot in selected departments, subject to Finance validation of the baseline and attribution rules. Track labour, F&B cost %, GOP and guest satisfaction; stop or redesign if service quality falls below the agreed threshold or if benefits cannot be reconciled to validated drivers.
