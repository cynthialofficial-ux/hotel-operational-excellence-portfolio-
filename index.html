Approach

2

Test

Compared Base / Moderate / Stretch scenarios across occupancy, ADR, labour and cost-driver levers, with service guardrails.

3

Govern

Applied ROI / NPV and sensitivity analysis, Finance validation, baseline and attribution controls, and a benefits tracker.

Executive conclusion

Near-budget revenue was not converting to planned GOP. The model isolated labour and F&B as priority cost drivers and recommended a controlled Moderate pilot, with Finance validation and a guest-satisfaction stop/redesign guardrail.
